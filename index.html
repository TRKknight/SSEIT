Emotional Intelligence Questionnaire

This short survey contains 33 statements about how you notice, understand, and use emotions. It takes about 5 minutes.

  • Anonymous. No names, emails, or account details are collected.
  • Voluntary. You can stop at any point before submitting.
  • Instant result. Your score is shown as soon as you submit.

For each statement, choose how strongly you agree, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). Answer as you generally are, not how you think you should be.
